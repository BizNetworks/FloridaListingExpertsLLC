Questions About Working with a Florida Real Estate Agency

What does a real estate agency in Florida do for sellers?

It prices your home using recent comparable sales, markets it across major listing platforms and social channels, manages showings and offers, and guides you through the contract and closing.

Can you help me buy from out of state?

Yes. Many of our clients relocate from the Northeast or abroad. We can arrange virtual tours, explain local costs such as insurance, and coordinate moving services.

Do you handle luxury and private sales?

We do. For high-net-worth clients we offer a confidential approach and marketing that complies with current MLS rules while protecting your privacy.

How do I find out what my home is worth?

Call us for a home valuation. We compare your property with nearby sales and current buyer demand to suggest a realistic list price.

Are commissions negotiable?

Commissions are set by agreement and put in writing before you sign. We are happy to walk you through exactly what is included.
